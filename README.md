


<center> <h2>Hello👋 my name is Jeffery</h2> </center>

I'm a physiologist and software developer based in Winnipeg. My MSc in Physiology & Pathophysiology at the University of Manitoba focused on [a 3D-bioprinted airway smooth muscle model for studying asthma](https://mspace.lib.umanitoba.ca/items/f5b6aa9f-d968-4e65-a698-c0abaf1e048b).

During my master's, I used the Cytation 5 to photograph bioprinted airways before and after adding contractile agents. I had to trace the lumen in each image by hand to calculate the percentage reduction in its area. It was tedious and prone to error, so I started looking into automating it.

That led me to programming. From the moment I opened a terminal window, I knew there was no going back. I wanted to understand how software worked and build other things too, so I kept learning and eventually landed in full-stack development.

The original idea became [BioRing Analyzer](https://github.com/egbonjefri/bioring_analyzer), which uses TensorFlow.js and OpenCV.js to detect bioprinted airway rings and measure changes in lumen area in the browser. I've also built a [bio-ink calculator](https://github.com/egbonjefri/bioink_calculator) and a [stroke prediction app](https://github.com/egbonjefri/stroke-risk-prediction-app) using R and Plumber.

I work mainly with Java, Python, R and JavaScript/TypeScript and I'm currently designing a small processor using only NOR gates, with a longer-term idea in mind: building computers out of proteins.

I love logic and philosophy, which sometimes means debating Parmenides versus Kant with Claude at 3 in the morning about what we actually mean by "change". I also play chess and love puzzle games like Portal and The Talos Principle.
